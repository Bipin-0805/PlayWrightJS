# Interview Preparation

## Question 1 : Tell me about yourself and your experience as a QA Engineer ?

Hi, my name is Bipin Sharma, and I am from Gwalior. I have over four years of experience in Software Quality Assurance, primarily in Manual Testing of web and mobile applications.

During my professional career, I have worked on three major projects.

My first project was **AxelGo**, a decentralized cloud-based application used for sharing, storing, and fetching files. I was involved in testing its web, mobile, and desktop applications.

My second project was **LetMeSee**, a photo and video-sharing application, where I primarily worked on mobile application testing.

My third and most recent project was **Axel Market**, a US-based e-commerce platform available on both web and mobile. I have spent approximately 1.5 to 2 years working on this project.

My key responsibilities included understanding requirements, designing and executing test cases, performing smoke and regression testing, identifying defects, and reporting them through Jira. I also coordinated with developers to get defects resolved, retested fixes, and verified the bug tickets before closure.

I have experience working with Agile methodologies and test management tools like TestLink and Jira. Additionally, I have learned Java and Selenium for automation testing, along with SQL for database validation.

I am now looking forward to an opportunity where I can utilize my manual testing experience while expanding my knowledge of API testing, automation, and cloud-based application testing.
